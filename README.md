## B. Synopsis
Fast & Furious is an action-packed film franchise that centers on Dominic Toretto, Brian O'Conner, and their closely-knit team of skilled drivers. What begins as an underground street racing saga in Los Angeles gradually transforms into a high-stakes series of global heist and espionage missions. Beneath the adrenaline-fueled car chases, explosive stunts, and dangerous villains, the core theme of the story remains deeply rooted in the concept of loyalty and family. The crew repeatedly places their lives on the line for one another, proving that their bond transcends mere friendship or criminal partnership. Ultimately, the franchise captivates audiences worldwide by combining fast-paced vehicular action with a powerful message about trust, unit, and home.
